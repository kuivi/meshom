# m12 — cubed sphere moved by optimal transport (`m12_cubed_ot`, `m12_cubed_ot_polish`)

- **What.** Equiangular cubed sphere, n = round(√(∫h⁻²dA/6)) cells per face edge; nodes moved with fixed topology, x = exp_ξ(∇φ), by the fixed-point Monge–Ampère iteration of Weller, Browne, Budd and Cullen 2016 (J. Comput. Phys. 308, 102–123, doi:10.1016/j.jcp.2015.12.018; eqs. 22, 34): P1 cotangent Laplacian on both quad diagonals, factorised once; target quad area c·h(centroid)² (not scaled by the computational cell, so the grid's 1.37× area spread goes too). Chosen over Winslow / variable diffusion: exact equidistribution at one sparse solve per iteration. Variant `_polish`: L-BFGS (JAX x64) on a TMOP-type corner energy Σ[(|e1|²+|e2|²)/(2a) − 1 + λ ln²(a/ch²)], λ = 1, before the coast cut.
- **Coast.** Move on the closed sphere; keep cells with all nodes in water and ≥ 0.4 h from the coast; clean pinches; one coastal row between the staircase and the resampled coast (merge walk: quad where both polylines advance, triangle where one does; all resampled coast nodes kept); 30 guarded Laplacian sweeps on 3 rows. `coast="snap"` (literal projection) gives ~180° corners on oblique coasts.
- **Works.** Uniform: 120° max (the 8 cube corners), Euler 8, 0.6 s at scale 1. Where OT converges the areas are exact (disc scale 1: |ln(√A/h)| p99 0.007). All runs valid (no inverted element, Euler ok) except the polish variant on continent at scale 4 (below).
- **Where it breaks (scale 4, closed sphere, quad θmax / min SJ vs size ratio).** Disc ×1.25 125°/0.82, ×1.5 136°/0.69, ×2 147°/0.49, ×3 160°/0.28, ×4 165°/0.22, ×5 178°/0.04 (tangles after ~120 iterations; the best untangled iterate is returned, area error up to 2.5×; converges at scale 1, still 178.6°). Band ×1.5 124°, ×2 132°, ×3 160°, ×4 165°. The 150° hard gate breaks between ×2 and ×3, the 135° p99.9 target at ×2.
- **Why (topological, not the solver).** Without new irregular nodes every grid line entering the fine region crosses its rim: the disc rim holds ≥ 2√(πN_in) ≈ 92 edges where 28 fit at h_out (scale 4) — tangential compression ×3.3, radial stretch, rhombi of up to 2·atan(k²) where grid lines are oblique. n3/n5/T stay 8/0/0 against ρ_c⁺/ρ_c⁻ = 26/−18 (disc), 94/−86 (band): distortion absorbs ρ_c.
- **Polish trade-off.** It moves the defect into size: disc ×2 λ=10/1/0.1 → 139/130/120.5° at size p99 0.02/0.14/0.42; ×3 λ=1 146° (0.16), ×5 λ=1 160°, λ=0.1 122° (0.53); band ×4 λ=1 152°, λ=0.1 131° (0.75). λ = 1 moves the 150° break from ~×2 to ~×3 at |ln(√A/h)| p99 ≈ 0.15.
- **Fails at line features.** The 15 km channel is filled by cells compressed across and stretched along it (~3:1), rhombi of 140–170°; coastal quads reach 172–175°. With the polish at scale 4 the squarer cells no longer fit the 2.5-cell channel: it closes (2 bridge elements, coast nodes kept 116/335, Euler 0 — FAIL); at scale 1 it stays open (Euler −4).
- **Scale 1 numbers** (raw; the competition runs, M3 Pro): disc 214k nodes, θmax 178.6/165.3 (max/p99.9), per-edge |ln s/h| p99 0.48, 48 s, 1.0 GB; band 421k, 164.5/163.6, 96 s, 1.4 GB; continent 196k, 0.3 % triangles, 173.5/165.7 (coast 171.9/169.5), 60 s, 1.1 GB. Polish: disc 160.2/155.4, band 165.5/154.2, continent 172.1/158.9, +30–60 s. Common `--smooth` changes little (disc 169.3/165.4). Gates: uniform 12/14, others 5–8 of 14/24.
- **Time.** The fixed-point iterations (400 = max_iter on disc/band, ~0.2 s each at 421k nodes: solve 0.08 s, areas + h 0.1 s); splu 4.9 s and operators 2.7 s once.
- **Production would need.** Not a fill method above ~×1.5–2 or for line features. Useful as a global equidistribution pass for mild variation (×≤1.5) on a structured grid, and its TMOP energy as a fixed-topology size+shape polish for other methods' meshes; that would need monitor smoothing, a Newton/cofactor linearisation (fewer iterations, no tangling at ×5) and a boundary-fitted start instead of cut-and-strip.
